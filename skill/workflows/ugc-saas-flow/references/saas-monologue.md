# Site monologue — hook → the site solves it → result + action

Write in **English always** (Seedance mangles other languages); only switch on an explicit ask.
The story sells the outcome of USING the site — never an object in hands during the body.

## Structure

| Part | Share | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (first 1–2s) | ~8-12% | Bold specific claim, ≤8 words, one clause, WITH a hook token (number / price / % / time / contrast / named result). No greeting, no filler opener. |
| Body | ~70% | Walk the useful sections in card order. **First body beat names the site/brand** ("so I opened Meller…") — the hero card anchors there, not at the hook. One concrete line per section while its card is up. |
| Closer (last ~15%) | ~15% | Concrete result (number/time) + CTA, while the creator does the product action: physical → picks it up (allowed here only); SaaS/service → acts on the phone. |

Hook and site-naming are two DIFFERENT beats — never merge them.
Skip filler sections (footer, logo strip): no line, no card.

**Hook angles** (pick one, write your own): price contrast ("$65 sunglasses that look like $300") ·
secret/reveal · social-proof number ("3 million people bought these") · "Stop X" conflict ·
transformation/result ("This site saved me 20 hours a week") · speed ("Found my pair in 60 seconds").
Banned openers: `OK/So/Wait/Um/Well/Like` and pointing intros (`These are / This is / Here's /
Meet / I found / Let me show`). Bold but plausible — never scammy.

## Sound spoken, not written

- ~**2.7–3.0 words/sec**, and VARY the speed within the take — quicker on connective lines, slower
  to land the number/price/CTA. A constant pace reads robotic.
- Contractions always; 1–2 conversational connectors (`ok so`, `honestly`, `no joke`, `and get
  this`) — never as the hook's first word; varied sentence lengths (clipped 2–4 word lines mixed
  with longer); ONE live imperfect beat per monologue (micro self-correction / aside).
- Every claim carries one concrete (number, time, named comparison); praise without a concrete is
  cut. Banned: `literally, obsessed, game-changer, holy grail, hits different` + corporate filler
  (`elevate / seamless / effortless / synergy / leverage / unlock / supercharge / revolutionary`).
- Body words describe the REAL card on screen — never promise UI the capture doesn't show.

## Word density

≤10s ~27-30 words · 11-12s ~32-36 · 13-15s ~40-45; budget across hook/body/closer by the share
table. Don't exceed (~3.5 wps tips into gabbling).

## Self-check before generating (fail any → rewrite)

Hook: ≤8 words? token present? no banned opener? not restating the visual?
Monologue: sounds alive read aloud? contractions? a connector? varied length AND pace?

## Output

Full monologue text; hook + closer segments with word counts (closer notes the product action);
body as ordered `{section_label, words}` — one entry per captured still (~6-10), in card order.
